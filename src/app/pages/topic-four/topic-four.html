Faculty of Dentistry, University of Szeged

Hungary: Dental education was introduced in the University of Szeged in 1960. The School of Dentistry was transformed into the Faculty of Dentistry in 2007. The English-language dental program was launched in the academic year of 2004/2005.

This programme is designed to unify the basic and clinical sciences, as it is believed that scientific and professional development cannot be separated but should proceed concurrently throughout the programme. In the first three years students are taught the basic sciences of medicine and dentistry. From the second year on, there is more emphasis on the study of dental diseases and their treatment. The last two years serve as the intensive clinical study of each of the various disciplines of dentistry. In the final year students participate in elective programmes, clinical conferences and hospital-based trainings.

The Faculty of Dentistry is the regional centre established for dental care in the southeastern region of Hungary. The graduates of the University are entitled to apply for hospital residency positions worldwide. Citizens of the EU will receive their licenses automatically within the Community.

• Level of the programme: undivided master's programme
• Title acquired: Doctor Medicinae Dentaire
• Duration of the programme: 10 semesters
• Registered in: EU, Credits 300

Titles by countries

USA, Canada: DDS (Doctor of Dental Surgery) or DMD (Doctor of Medicine) degree - have Dr. in front of their names. D.M.D. and D.D.S. programs are different only in name. They signify the same capabilities to practice dentistry and use identical curriculum requirements. Candidates must complete a bachelor's degree first before applying to the 4-year dental school.

UK, Australia: BDS/BChD/BDentS (Bachelor of Dental Surgery); later on (3 year postgraduate specialisation): MDS/MChD (Master of Dental Surgery). Master of Dentistry courses take 1-5 years. Most universities strongly prefer that applicants have at least two years of clinical experience as a practicing dentist before applying.

Other Nations: Germany: Doctor of Dentistry (Dr. med. dent.) | Brazil: Doutor em Odontologia | France: Docteur en Chirurgie Dentaire.
